## QanunOS Vision hypothesis
Combine a narrow fictional oral-argument rehearsal with task-positioned spatial cues, explicit rejection of unsupported references, and a short source-inspection interaction. Use a deterministic Egyptian-authority fixture with provenance that the viewer can inspect. This combination is the proposed difference; it is not yet demonstrated or proven unique.

## Falsifiable comparison
Use the same fictional question and the same curated references in a laptop-note baseline and the Lens preview. Check whether the participant can answer, identify the unsupported reference, inspect the replacement source and return to rehearsal. Record omissions, navigation burden and correctness. Any timing numbers require an actual measured protocol; preview results cannot establish comfort or tracking quality on real glasses.

If the Lens is simply a longer teleprompter script, or if the rejection comes from a scripted animation rather than actual validation, the proposed mechanism is not demonstrated. Reduce the scope until source identity checking and inspection are real.

## Remaining evidence
No direct competitor trial, user interview, independent novelty review, measured advantage or physical-glasses study exists. No first-of-its-kind claim is warranted.
