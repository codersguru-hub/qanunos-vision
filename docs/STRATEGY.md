# QanunOS Vision strategy

Decision: GO for review/recording of the verified local prototype; Gate 0 and Gate 2 passed. Submission remains NO-GO until the actual video/public materials and final required checks exist. This is a strategy assessment, not a forecast of winning.

## 15-second pitch
QanunOS Vision helps lawyers rehearse with their eyes up. In a fictional hearing, a spatial cue shows a traceable legal authority; an unsupported citation is visibly rejected before it can enter the argument.

## Narrow slice
One fictional scenario, one prepared mock-judge trigger, one rejected fictional citation, one verified replacement, source reveal and return. Use no real courtroom audio or client records. Prefer deterministic local fixtures before any network integration. Show fixture and mock-interaction labels.

The distinctive hypothesis is that a small source card positioned near the rehearsal context lets the lawyer inspect a citation without switching to a laptop. That advantage and novelty remain unproven until preview testing and comparison research. Do not claim first-of-its-kind.

## Build sequence
1. Clear registration and per-project rules review.
2. Verify the real MCP loaded project/account readiness, then complete official plan-lens and participant design sign-off; retain the child project's devpost/design-brief.md.
3. Prove Lens Studio + CLAD + MCP using one visible spatial card and observed select/dismiss interaction before full implementation.
4. Implement the smallest complete interaction; verify English/Arabic text rendering before expanding.
5. Curate source-checked legal fixtures and reject missing, mismatched or unsupported citations.
6. Capture a 90-second uninterrupted preview run, then a polished 150-second video.
7. Perform a disclosed skeptical self-review, refresh official requirements, publish only authorized materials and submit only after explicit confirmation.

All beats are plans. None has been executed. A clearly labeled mock trigger is acceptable for the rehearsal story; it must never be narrated as working speech recognition.

## Skeptical self-review
- Design: Could the same benefit be achieved more comfortably with a laptop? Test placement, reading burden and hands-free return.
- Technical: Does full article identity validation reject suffix mismatches and unknown IDs? Require observed tests.
- Innovation: What does the mechanism add beyond notes in glasses? Research nearest alternatives and show source inspection in context.
- Presentation: Does the footage prove a working Lens and distinguish fixtures from live integration? Record actual preview.

## Scope and unknowns
No CRM, signup, payments, broad RAG platform, real courtroom deployment, or office briefing before the main slice passes.
Participant hours and spending budget are unknown. Do not buy services. Record a realistic schedule and reserve after toolchain setup.
QanunOS is pre-existing infrastructure and must be disclosed if used. Develop the Lens and any adapter from scratch.
